Features Overview

● Interactive Audio Visualizer

  • Real-time Signal Response: Waveforms, particles, and lines move with the music’s frequency and amplitude.
  • Theme Selection: Choose between neon, pastel, and dark modes for your visual flavor.
  • Customization Panel: Adjust particle count, line thickness, and color palettes on the fly.
  • Full-Screen Mode: Seamlessly expand to fill your display for immersive sessions.

● Financial Planning Suite

  • Savings Projection: Visualize your account growth over 1–10 year horizons with dynamic line charts.
  • Budget Analyzer: Enter income and expenses to receive a monthly surplus/deficit breakdown.
  • Goal Tracker: Set targets (e.g., $30 000 home down payment) and monitor your progress.
  • Scenario Simulator: Test “what-if” scenarios by tweaking interest rates, contribution amounts, or timeframes.

● 3D Three.js Visualizer (Coming Soon)

Experience audio-reactive 3D geometries:

  • Rotating spheres that pulse with bass drops.
  • Wireframe grids and shader effects synced to mid/high frequencies.
  • Camera transitions and orbit controls for interactive exploration.

● Utility & Accessibility

  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Spacebar to play/pause, ↑/↓ to adjust volume, F to toggle full-screen.
  • Dark & Light Themes: Auto-switch based on system preferences or manual toggle.
  • Screen Reader Support: ARIA labels on all controls and visual indicators.
  • Responsive Layout: Mobile-friendly menus and swipe gestures for playlists.

● Integration & Export

  • Playlist Import: Upload your own .m3u or .json playlists.
  • Snapshot Exports: Capture and download PNG snapshots of your visualizer frames.
  • CSV Export: Export financial simulation data for offline analysis.
  • API Access: Fetch raw data endpoints for custom front-ends or third-party apps.
