Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Synth & Financial Tools (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information in accordance with major data privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: If you register, we may collect your name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymized data about pages visited, time on site, clicks, errors, and device/browser information to improve our service.
  • Financial Inputs: All numbers entered into the financial calculator are processed in your browser and are not stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To analyze site usage and enhance user experience.
  • To comply with any legal obligations.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with essential service providers for functions like web hosting and analytics, under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, please contact us.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for basic site functionality. We may use analytics cookies to understand traffic, but we will ask for your consent before doing so where required by law.

6. Contact & Updates

For questions about this policy, please email us at stephenalfred31@gmail.com. We may update this policy periodically and will note the "Last Updated" date.
